Campaign concept

The 5 PM Reset

Turn the commute hour into a ritual. From 5 PM, Bloom becomes the place Salmiya's young professionals go to switch off before the evening starts: a slower cup, a calmer table, and a menu that only exists after work. Every piece of content points to one daily moment people can build a habit around.
